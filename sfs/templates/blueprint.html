How to load blueprint

  1. Download desired blueprint zip file. (BP Sharing is directly downloaded to your sfs game)
  2. Locate the file
  3. In your android device, extract the zip file and move to the following directory:
  4. Android > media > com.StefMorojna.SpaceflightSimulator > Savings > Blueprints >
  5. Paste the extracted folder here
  6. Then enjoy the blueprint in your game!
